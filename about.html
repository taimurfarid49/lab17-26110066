Our story

About Dera Crunch

Crunch Hai Toh Maza Hai

A "dera" is a place where people gather, share a meal and stay a little longer than planned. That's exactly the feeling we wanted, plus a lot of crunch.

How it started

Big crunch, warm welcome

Dera Crunch began with one simple idea: fried chicken should be loud, golden and shared. We imagined a spot where families squeeze into one table, doston argue over the last wing, and everyone leaves happy.

So we built a short menu and did it properly. Desi masalay in the marinade, a double coat for extra crunch, and sauces with just the right amount of tez. No fuss, no tension, sirf maza.

The crunch method

Three steps to maza

  1. Marinate overnight

    Chicken rests in yoghurt, lemon and our desi masala mix so every bite is full of flavour.

  2. Double-dip the crumb

    Two coats of spiced crumb build that signature, can-hear-it-across-the-room crunch.

  3. Fry till golden

    Cooked fresh and served garma garam, because thanda chicken is nobody's friend.

Four quick answers before your next crunch.

Is Dera Crunch a real restaurant?

Nahi! Dera Crunch is a made-up brand for a fictional student project. There are no real branches, deals or deliveries, so please don't go looking for us on the map.

How spicy is the "Dera Fire" level?

It's our hottest of three spice levels in Build Your Combo. Mild is nani approved, Medium brings a little tear, and Dera Fire calls for a Thandi Mint Lemonade (Rs. 280) on standby.

What is the Chef's Pick?

Every day the home page highlights one menu item, chosen from the date, for example the Double Mazay Burger on 23 Nov 2026. It's simply our favourite of the day at its normal menu price, not a discount or offer.

How should I write my phone number?

Use the format 03XX-XXXXXXX. Our order and contact forms only accept a number with exactly 11 digits, and they'll tell you politely if something is missing.
