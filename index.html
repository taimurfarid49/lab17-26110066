About us

A little dera with a big crunch

Dera Crunch is our happy hangout for crispy chicken and feel-good food. Think warm welcomes, loud crunches and a table full of family and yaar dost.

Every piece is marinated with desi masalay, double-coated for extra crunch and fried till perfectly golden. Simple food, big flavour, zero tension.
